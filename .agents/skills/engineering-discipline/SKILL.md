## Commits and PRs

One conventional commit per logical unit. Do not batch a whole plan into a single commit when the work splits. Subject: imperative, ~72 characters; body explains **why**. Never `--no-verify`.

Fill [`.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md`](../../../.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md). Do not replace it with Summary / Test plan. Title: conventional.
